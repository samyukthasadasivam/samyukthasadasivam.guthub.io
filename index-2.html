About Me

Where data meets story.

I'm Samyuktha, a marketing professional based in Chennai, India, with an Economics degree from Penn State. I care deeply about understanding what consumers actually want and why, and I bring that curiosity into every campaign I work on.

My strength is in the early stages of a campaign: reading consumer taste, spotting what will land, and brainstorming ideas that are both creative and practical. At Pickyourtrail, that looked like contributing to the PYT30in30 concept, a 30-reel sprint that kept audiences engaged with fresh destination content every single day. At ZF Group, it meant rethinking how a technical B2B product gets communicated and pitching a comic-strip format that made the story impossible to ignore.

I pay close attention to detail across every touchpoint, from the copy to the visual to the brief, because I think the small choices are what separate forgettable content from something that actually sticks. I'm most drawn to brands where consumer psychology, design, and culture all intersect, especially in beauty, lifestyle, and physical consumer goods.
