About Me

I'm the person in the room who notices when something could be better: the caption that's a little too safe, the campaign that's missing a hook, the brief that's technically right but creatively flat. I'm a Penn State Economics grad who ended up in marketing because I love people just as much as I love a good insight. I've pitched comic strips to automotive engineers, produced 30 reels in 30 days, and somehow made B2B safety tech feel like something you actually want to read about. I believe great marketing doesn't just reach people. It makes them feel something. That's what I'm here to make.
